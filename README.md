# MCP Reliability Index

An independent, pre-registered check of whether the tools of popular remote MCP servers do what their own descriptions say. Run by [Suncly](https://suncly.com).

## How it works
- **[METHOD.md](METHOD.md)**: the full method, published on 23 November 2026, before any data was collected. Any change after 23 November 2026 is recorded in its changelog with the date and the reason.
- **Release 1 (November 2026):** a ranking of public remote MCP servers from the official MCP Registry on what their tool lists declare: context cost, safety labels and undocumented inputs. No tools are called.
- **Release 2 (planned for mid-December 2026):** a behaviour check of read-only tools against their own descriptions. Every builder sees their results privately at least 10 days before publication.

## Results
Results, with the raw answers they are computed from, are published in [results/](results/), one folder per release.

## For server owners
- To ask for a re-read after you have changed your tool list, or to ask us to stop reading your server, email team@suncly.com.
- In Release 1 we only read what a server publicly declares, one request at a time, at least 1 second apart.
